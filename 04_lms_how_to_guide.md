# LMS How-To Guide

**Version 1.2 | Owner: Training Team | Fictional document for demo purposes**

## Logging in
Open the LMS from the company intranet and sign in with your work account using single sign-on. There is no separate LMS password. If sign-in fails, clear your browser cache and try again, then contact IT Service Desk.

## Finding your assigned courses
After logging in, select My Learning on the home page. Courses assigned to you appear under Required, and courses you chose yourself appear under Optional. Each course shows its due date.

## Completing an e-learning course
Select the course and choose Start. Progress saves automatically, so you can leave and resume later. The course is marked complete when you finish all modules and pass any final quiz.

## Downloading your certificate
Open My Learning, choose Completed, select the course and click Download Certificate. Certificates are available as PDF files. If the button is missing, the course may still be awaiting trainer confirmation, which takes up to 2 working days.

## Viewing your transcript
Go to My Profile and select Transcript to see all completed courses with dates and scores. You can export the transcript as PDF.

## Resetting a stuck course
If a module will not load or progress is not saving, try another browser first (Edge or Chrome are supported). If the problem continues, raise a ticket with the IT Service Desk and include the course name and a screenshot.

## Mobile access
The LMS works in a mobile browser. Some interactive modules are only supported on desktop.
