# Training Attendance Policy

**Version 2.1 | Owner: Training Team | Fictional document for demo purposes**

## 1. Purpose
This policy sets out what is expected of employees who register for instructor-led or virtual training sessions.

## 2. Attendance expectations
Employees must join each session on time. For virtual sessions, join the Teams meeting at least 5 minutes before the start time. Participants who arrive more than 15 minutes late may be marked as a No-show at the trainer's discretion.

Attendance requires staying for the full session. Cameras should be on during virtual sessions unless there is a bandwidth issue, which should be mentioned to the trainer in advance.

## 3. Recording attendance
Trainers record attendance within 1 working day after the session. Each registration is set to one of two final statuses: Attended or No-show. Only participants marked Attended receive the feedback form and any completion record.

## 4. No-shows
A No-show is a registered participant who neither attends nor cancels at least 24 hours before the session. After a No-show, the participant receives an email with the next available session and their line manager is notified. Three No-shows within 12 months are escalated to the department head.

## 5. Excused absences
Absences due to sick leave, approved annual leave, or urgent business needs are excused if the employee informs the Training Team before the session or within 1 working day afterwards. Excused absences are not counted as No-shows.

## 6. Reminders
Participants receive an automatic reminder email at 9 AM on the day before their session, including the Teams link for virtual sessions.
