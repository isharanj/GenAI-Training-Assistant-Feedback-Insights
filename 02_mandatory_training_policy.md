# Mandatory Training Policy

**Version 1.4 | Owner: Compliance and Training Team | Fictional document for demo purposes**

## 1. Purpose
Certain courses are required for all employees to meet legal, security and safety obligations.

## 2. Mandatory courses
All employees must complete the following:
- Information Security Awareness (annually)
- Code of Conduct and Ethics (every 2 years)
- Data Protection Essentials (annually)
- Workplace Health and Safety (every 3 years)

## 3. Deadlines
New joiners must complete Information Security Awareness and Code of Conduct within 30 days of their start date. Existing employees must renew each course before the expiry date shown in the LMS. The LMS sends renewal notices 60 days, 30 days and 7 days before expiry.

## 4. Overdue training
A course is overdue the day after its expiry or deadline date. Overdue employees are listed in the monthly compliance report sent to line managers. Employees who remain overdue for more than 30 days may have access to non-essential systems restricted until they complete the course.

## 5. Exemptions
Exemptions apply only for long-term leave of more than 90 days (for example parental or medical leave). The employee must complete the overdue courses within 30 days of returning. Exemption requests are approved by the Training Lead and recorded in the LMS.

## 6. Responsibilities
Employees complete their courses on time. Line managers review the monthly report and follow up. The Training Team maintains the course list and the reports.
