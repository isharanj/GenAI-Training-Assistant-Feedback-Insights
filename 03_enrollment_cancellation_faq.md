# Enrollment and Cancellation FAQ

**Version 1.3 | Owner: Training Team | Fictional document for demo purposes**

## How do I register for a session?
Fill in the Training Registration form shared on the Training Team site and pick the session from the list. You will receive a confirmation email and a calendar invite within a few minutes.

## What if a session is full?
You are added to the waitlist and receive a waitlist email. If a seat opens because someone cancels, the Training Team contacts waitlisted participants in the order they registered.

## How many sessions can I register for at once?
You can hold up to 3 active registrations at the same time. Contact the Training Team if you need more.

## How do I cancel?
Reply to your confirmation email or message the Training Team at least 24 hours before the session. Cancelling frees your seat for the waitlist. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the session are treated as No-shows unless the absence is excused.

## Can I swap to a different date?
Yes. Cancel your current registration at least 24 hours before and register for the new date through the form. There is no fee for rescheduling.

## Can my manager register me?
Yes. Managers can submit the form on behalf of a team member using the team member's name and work email.

## Is there a cost to attend?
Internal training sessions are free for employees. Training for external certifications needs line manager approval and a cost centre code.

## Who do I contact for help?
Contact the Training Team through the Training Team channel in Teams or by email to the training mailbox.
