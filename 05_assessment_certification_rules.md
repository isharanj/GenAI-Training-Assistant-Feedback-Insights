# Assessment and Certification Rules

**Version 1.1 | Owner: Training Team | Fictional document for demo purposes**

## 1. Passing mark
Assessments are scored out of 100. The pass mark is 80% for mandatory courses and 70% for all other courses.

## 2. Attempts
Participants have 3 attempts per assessment. After a failed attempt, a participant must wait 24 hours before trying again. After 3 failed attempts, the participant must retake the course content and the Training Team resets the assessment.

## 3. Time limits
Timed assessments allow 45 minutes unless stated in the course description. Answers are submitted automatically when time runs out.

## 4. Certificates
A certificate is issued automatically after a passing score and confirmed attendance for instructor-led sessions. Certificates show the course title, completion date and expiry date where applicable.

## 5. Validity and renewal
Mandatory course certificates are valid for the period in the Mandatory Training Policy. Other certificates do not expire unless the course description says so. Renewals follow the same assessment rules.

## 6. Reasonable adjustments
Participants who need adjustments (extra time, alternative formats) should contact the Training Team at least 5 working days before the assessment. Adjustments are handled confidentially.

## 7. Academic integrity
Assessments must be completed individually. Sharing answers or using unapproved AI tools during an assessment is a breach of the Code of Conduct and may lead to the result being cancelled.
